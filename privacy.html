Privacy Policy — RD Quiz

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

RD Quiz does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any personal information. Your best scores are stored only on your own device. The current web version shows demo ads only and does not track you.

2. Advertising

The native mobile version will show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This policy will be updated when real ads are enabled.

3. Third-Party Services

We use no analytics in the current version. Links to external sites (for example, Young Bl4ck News) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

RD Quiz is a general-audience trivia game and does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access or delete. You can clear your scores at any time by clearing the app's stored data in your browser.
